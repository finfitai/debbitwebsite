Questions

What people ask us first.

Do I need to know accounting?

No. uses plain words like “Owed to you” and “You owe”. It keeps proper books for you in the background, and your accountant gets full reports on their own screen.

Which one is right for me?

One shop and a small team: . Several branches, departments or more than a handful of people who need their own logins: . Want experts to fix how your business runs: . Not sure? Book a call and we will tell you honestly.

Does it do e-invoicing?

Yes. sends e-invoices to MyInvois in Malaysia and to ZATCA in Saudi Arabia, and shows the status of each one.

Does it file my tax return?

No. prepares the figures from your own records. You or your accountant file the return.

Can my staff have their own logins?

Yes. In each person is invited by email and gets their own permissions. In each person gets their own PIN and role.

Can a staff member delete a sale?

No. A posted sale is corrected with a refund or credit note, so there is always a clear record.

Does it use AI on my data?

Not today. When AI features arrive they will be switched off until you turn them on, they will ask before acting, and they will never move money or message your customers on their own.
