Professional rules come first

Partner with us, within your rules.

Chartered accountants work under codes of ethics that can limit referral fees and commissions. We designed the program so every partner can take part properly.

  • Always disclosed

    Any referral share is written in your partner agreement and disclosed to the client. We never offer hidden commissions or incentives meant to sway your independent judgement.

  • A non-commission path

    If your rules limit referral income, or you would rather not take it, choose training, free accountant tools, technical support and a subscription discount passed to your client instead.

  • Your clients, your judgement

    Recommend only where it suits the client. Audit and assurance clients may need extra care under independence rules. We will not ask you to do anything your code forbids.

  • MY
    Malaysia

    MIA members follow the MIA By-Laws on Professional Ethics, Conduct and Practice, which address fees, referral fees and commissions.

  • SA
    Saudi Arabia

    SOCPA members follow SOCPA's code of professional conduct, based on the international code of ethics for professional accountants.

  • IN
    India

    ICAI members are bound by the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949, which restricts sharing fees, commission and brokerage. Practising CAs default to the non-commission path.

Eligibility for any referral share is subject to local professional rules and your own professional body's guidance. This page is not legal or ethics advice.

Questions

Partner questions.

Are you trying to replace accountants?

No. keeps the day-to-day records in one place so you can spend your time on review, tax and advice. We never market it as a replacement for an accountant.

Who handles demos and support for my clients?

We do. You introduce the client, and our team runs the demo, sets the business up and answers product questions. Certified Partners may also charge their own fees for setup and advisory work.

Are the commission figures final?

No. They are proposed terms for the program. Final terms will be in a written partner agreement, and any referral share is disclosed to the client.

I can't accept commissions. Can I still partner?

Yes. Choose the non-commission path: training and certification, free accountant tools, technical support and a subscription discount for your clients.

Which countries does this cover?
