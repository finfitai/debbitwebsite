Who is responsible for your information

For the website, for your account and for booking calls with us, debbit decides how your information is used.

When a business uses debbit to keep its own records (sales, invoices, stock, customers, suppliers, staff), that business decides what goes in and how it is used. For those records debbit acts on the business's instructions. If you are a customer, supplier or employee of a business that uses debbit, please contact that business first about your information.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, password (stored by our sign-in provider, never in plain text) and the business you belong to.
  • Business records: whatever you and your team enter or import, such as products, prices, sales, invoices, payments, stock, customers, suppliers and staff details.
  • Messages to us: what you send through the contact form, by email, or in a support request, and the details you give when you book a call.
  • Payment details: if you pay us by card, the card is handled by our payment provider. We see only a summary such as the card brand and last four digits.
  • Technical information: your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages and features you use, kept in server logs so the service stays secure and working.
  • Your choices on this website: the language and light or dark look you pick are kept in your own browser.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service you signed up for and keep your records available to you.
  • To sign you in and keep your account and your business's data secure.
  • To answer your questions, help you set up and provide support.
  • To take payment and keep the records the law asks us to keep.
  • To fix problems and improve debbit.
  • To tell you about important changes to the service. We send marketing only where you have agreed, and you can stop it at any time.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your business records to advertise to you or anyone else.

The AI features

Some debbit features use AI, for example asking a question about your business in plain words. When you use them, the question and the records needed to answer it are sent to our AI provider to produce the answer. Our AI provider does not use this information to train its models. AI features can only read what your account is allowed to see, and anything that would change your records needs a person's approval first.
